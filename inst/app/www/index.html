Welcome to CalciumInsights

An interactive platform for denoising, visualizing, and quantifying calcium transient signals.

About the Application

CalciumInsights is designed to support the analysis of calcium imaging signals through an interactive and user-friendly interface. The application allows users to upload calcium signal datasets, select specific regions of interest, apply signal smoothing methods, detect calcium transients, and extract relevant quantitative metrics such as peak amplitude, prominence, rise time, transient duration, full width at half maximum, and area under the curve.

1

Signal Denoising

Smooth calcium traces using frequency-based filtering methods while preserving the main morphology of the signal.

2

Transient Detection

Identify relevant peaks and calcium events using configurable parameters such as peak height, prominence, and minimum distance.

3

Metric Extraction

Generate quantitative summaries for calcium dynamics, including amplitude, rise time, FWHM, FWHP, AUC, and frequency-related metrics.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Upload your calcium signal dataset or use the example data provided in the app.
  2. Select the region of interest that you want to analyze.
  3. Adjust the denoising and peak detection parameters according to your signal behavior.
  4. Review the calcium trace, detected peaks, derivative plots, and raw-versus-smoothed comparison.
  5. Download the transient metrics, trace metrics, or calcium trace graph for reporting and further analysis.
